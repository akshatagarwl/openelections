# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
Delegated in the brief and confirmed through the decision page: lightweight Vite, accessible HTML, custom interactive diagrams.

## Users
Citizens, journalists and technical readers who can scrutinise and share a nonpartisan demand for open-source release of the relevant electoral-roll components.

## Product Purpose
OpenElections.in explicitly demands that ECI open-source the relevant parts of ECINet/ERONet: application-validation rules, official permissions and voter-restoration workflows, with supporting dependencies and audit evidence. The user's follow-up grounds this scope in three supplied Indian Express articles about Form 6, restricted officer access and Goa restoration failures. The documentary explains and substantiates the demand; education is a supporting function, not the site's final purpose. Nonpartisan does not mean neutral about publication.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Primary-source citations for factual claims. Clearly distinguish ECI statements, analysis, unknowns and advocacy demands. No allegation of manipulation or assertion of an existing legal source-publication duty. No petition, tracking, personal data collection or official affiliation. Fast, responsive and accessible. The website itself is MIT-licensed. The demand is for relevant electoral-roll components to be open-source for public auditing, not for every ECINet service to be released indiscriminately. Distinguish source availability from licensing permissions to run, modify and redistribute code. Exclude voter/staff data, credentials, private keys and live operational secrets.

## Brand Commitments
Interactive investigative documentary. Minimal text, strong typography, diagrams, scroll-driven motion and calm technical credibility. User-selected hero wording: “Make source code of ECINet/ERONet public.” Use the real public repository https://github.com/akshatagarwl/openelections for website source, rather than a ZIP download.

## Evidence on Hand
ECI releases published by PIB, Estonian election authority documentation and Swiss Federal Chancellery examination materials. Three user-supplied Indian Express articles: an original investigation, an explainer and an editorial. Label reporting as reporting and opinion as opinion; do not pretend this site possesses the internal notes. A review announcement is not a completed audit. An unavailable public artifact is not evidence of wrongdoing.

## Product Principles
- Make claims testable, not partisan.
- Protect personal data while explaining public-interest software disclosure.
- Separate documented facts from demands; state the demand clearly rather than softening it into an optional proposal.
- Keep citations adjacent and accessible.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Keyboard usable, semantic structure, reduced-motion support and responsive mobile reading. WCAG AA target.
