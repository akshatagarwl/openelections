## ECINet notes

- **26 Sep 2026 statement (PIB 2315309).** Item iv reads: "Field officers have role-based access to the ECINET system as per their statutory powers. A Committee headed by Sr. Deputy Election Commissioner (DEC) and including an independent expert from IIT/IIIT will review the ECINET to double-check that it complies with the provisions of the Acts and Rules and submit report to the Commission. Various upgradations of the portal have already been made based on the inputs from State CEOs in the past few months." The same statement says BLOs upload documents "on the ECINet, for decision by the ERO".
- **"Decentralised."** This claim comes only from PTI and IANS reports (24 Sep 2026) quoting unnamed officials "citing official documents". We found no signed ECI document containing that word, which is why the page labels it as a press report.
- **Context.** The review followed press reports (The Indian Express, Sep 2026) of internal objections within the Commission. The page does not repeat those allegations. It reports only what the Commission itself stated.
